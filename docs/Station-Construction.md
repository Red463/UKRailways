# Station construction set

54 construction blocks plus the Station Wayfinding Sign.

Platform pieces offer separate coping/paving and compact combined edges. Models use cardinal placement and Create wrench rotation/pickup. Holding a matching lamp post or canopy column and right-clicking an existing section extends its top, retaining its facing. Sneak-click for ordinary placement. Slabs support top/bottom/double placement and waterlogging; double slabs return two items. Thin structural parts support waterlogging. Railings connect as fences.

Bench sections join when adjacent and facing the same way; armrests remain at the ends. Right-click to sit using Create seating. Each placed piece is independent when removed. Lamps and strip lights emit level 15 light. Stack posts and columns, extend beams and repeat roof panels to build structures to your preferred size.

Shelter entrance frames repeat vertically for player-height openings. Help points use a round white housing, blue title, speaker grille and raised green Emergency/blue Information buttons inspired by real station units. The Classic Station Litter Bin is a separate freestanding black-and-gold unit with four openings and decorative LITTER panels. The bag litter bin is a black open hoop with a translucent hanging bag and small decorative contents. Help points and poster cases are decorative. The black wayfinding sign uses the existing text editor and a Cycle arrow button; mount on walls, poles or ceilings.

Textures are original stylised material patterns. Engineering brick uses faceted comic-style artwork with dark mortar while keeping its dark engineering palette; all retaining/coping/combined models share that texture. Native gameplay/shader/multiplayer acceptance remains pending.

## Help point placement

The same Help Point item supports floor and wall placement. Place on the ground for the short post/foot version, or on a horizontal wall face for the flush circular unit without a stand. Existing placed help points stay freestanding. Both mounts keep waterlogging and Create wrench rotation/pickup. The unit remains decorative station furniture.

## Canopy fascia corners

Modern and traditional fascia each have inside and outside corner pieces. Place them in the layer immediately below the roof, replacing the straight fascia at the elbow; rotate with the Create wrench to line up both legs. Continue with straight fascia along each edge. Traditional corners keep the same scallop height and scale. Each trim, light or beam needs its own block cell; move any light/beam occupying the new perimeter trim cell. All four pieces support waterlogging and sneak-wrench pickup.

## Bench corners

Matching bench types join in straight rows and automatic L corners. For an example, place an elbow bench facing north, another facing north immediately east of it, and one facing east immediately north of it. The elbow fills the turn, with armrests only at the exposed ends. Rotate this arrangement for other corners; placement order does not matter. Different bench types stay separate. Right-click any section to sit; waterlogging and wrench pickup remain supported.

## Suggested build order

1. Build retaining walls, asphalt/paving and coping. Rotate edge pieces so the white coping faces the track; place tactile paving and yellow lines behind it, or use combined edges.
2. Add stairs/ramps and railings, then benches and lighting.
3. Stack columns, then put beams/brackets above them. Roof panels sit at the bottom of their block so they meet the supports below. Put fascia in the row immediately below exposed roof edges. Mount strip lights in cells immediately below roof panels.
4. Build shelters from stacked glass/frame/corner modules and roof panels. Leave at least two entrance sections of clear height.
5. Add wayfinding, poster cases, bins, help points, drains and covers.

Selectable adverts are available in three additional wall-mounted board sizes. Every board offers twenty illustrated campaigns through its right-click selector; see [Station Advert Boards](Station-Adverts.md). The original decorative Station Poster Case is retained.
