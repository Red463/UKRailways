# Road and car-park asphalt markings

35 marking styles, each as a full block and slab (70 retained item IDs). All use the approved asphalt. Markings are decorative.

## Getting designs

Put plain or marked asphalt into the Roadworks Workbench and select a design. Each full block converts one-for-one within the full-block family; slabs convert one-for-one within their separate slab family. Colours are free to change. Craft plain slabs from three plain full blocks for six slabs before choosing markings. Existing blocks and inventories remain compatible.

## Placement and automatic end bars

Rotate with the Create wrench. Sneak-wrench picks up a piece. Slabs retain upper/lower/double placement, waterlogging and two-item double-slab drops. Single yellow, double yellow and double red lines add automatic exposed end bars; matching surface heights connect.

## Building layouts

Parking straight/corner/T/end pieces form open bays. Rotate corner and end pieces to choose the opening. Symbols/arrows have whole-tile and matching Left Half / Right Half versions. For a symbol centred on a two-block road seam, put the Left Half on the left and Right Half on the right when looking down on north-facing tiles, with both pieces facing the same way and at the same surface height. Rotate both pieces together for other road directions. The pair preserves the whole symbol size; only its position changes. Both halves come as full blocks and slabs. White centre/edge lines, zig-zags, zebra stripes, give-way marks and stop lines are separate modular pieces. Their joins follow the artwork; white markings do not receive automatic restriction end bars.
