# Station signs

Four decorative signs are available in the UK Railways creative tab and through
the recipes in [CRAFTING.md](Crafting.md).

- **Classic Station Sign:** red double-arrow totem with a white station-name strip.
- **Modern Station Sign:** wide white nameboard with an orange lower stripe and
  an original UK Railways roundel.
- **Platform Sign:** white panel with large black lettering. It accepts text such
  as `1`, `2A` or `Bay 3`, as well as station names or other short messages.
- **Local Railway Information Board:** fixed, fictional notices and a network
  guide in a freestanding frame. One item places a two-block-wide, two-block-high board.

## Edit and mount a sign

Place any of the three editable signs, then right-click with an **empty hand**.
Enter one line of up to 64 characters and choose **Done**. Case, spaces,
punctuation and Unicode are retained; text shrinks to fit. Short labels remain
easier to read. Editing follows the existing permission, distance and loaded-block
checks, and the text is saved with the world and synchronized to other players.
Lettering is printed on the front face.

For a pole mount, hold the sign and click a **horizontal side of a Wide Pole
without sneaking**. The sign occupies that pole cell and retains its continuous
pole section, so poles can continue above and below it. Breaking the mounted sign
returns the sign and the consumed pole in survival. The same floor, ceiling,
wall and Create-girder mounting system used by existing signs is supported.

## Fixed information board

Place it on two adjacent solid supports with a clear 2x2 space above. It faces you and
extends to its right, placing all four quarters automatically; it cannot be mounted to a pole or edited.
Breaking any quarter removes the complete structure and returns one board in
survival. Removing its support also removes it. It is decorative printed artwork,
separate from the live amber **Station Information Board**, and needs no station link.

## Stop Look Listen Sign
The fixed warning sign reads Stop / Look / Listen / Beware / of trains. Right-click a wall face to mount it using the rear bracket. Right-click the side of a Wide Pole with the sign to mount it in the pole block; avoid sneaking during placement. Normal wrench use rotates the sign; sneak-wrench picks it up. An integrated pole drops alongside the sign when removed. Floor and ceiling supports are also available. The lettering is fixed.
Crafting is shapeless: iron ingot, paper, red dye and black dye give one sign.

The 2x2 board uses one item and drops one item. Sneak-wrench any quarter to collect it; normal wrench rotation checks the new footprint for space, support and permissions. Pick up existing narrow boards before updating, then place them again with sufficient room. Existing narrow structures are not automatically expanded. Updated 2x2 preview.
