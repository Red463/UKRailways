# Tunnel Bulkhead Light

A compact round, wall-mounted tunnel light with charcoal casing, silver protective cage and warm ivory diffuser. It is always on and emits light level 15. Vanilla light is uncoloured; warm-coloured illumination depends on shaders.

Place it by clicking a horizontal face of a sturdy wall. It cannot be placed on floors or ceilings. It supports waterlogging and drops when its wall support is removed. Create sneak-wrench collects it; normal wrench turns it 90 degrees when the new facing has a sturdy wall behind it. The fitting is approximately 10/16 of a block across and 3.35/16 deep.

Craft two using:

```text
.            Iron ingot   .
Iron bars    Glass pane   Iron bars
.            Glowstone    .
```

Use a pickaxe to mine it. Install the updated mod on server and clients. Actual model preview; [complete crafting guide](Crafting.md).
