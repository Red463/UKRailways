# Station advert boards

There are three wall-mounted board items: Small (1x2 blocks), Large (2x3), and Landscape (4x2). Every size offers all twenty approved fictional Minecraft adverts. Only the three frame items appear in the main creative/JEI catalogue.

Use the board item on the bottom-left area of a solid wall. The whole rectangle needs clear space in front and solid support behind it. Right-click any part to open the illustrated selector, choose an advert, then press **Apply**. Cancel leaves the current advert unchanged.

The selected advert survives saving and reloading. Sneak-right-click any part with the Create wrench to retrieve the complete board as one configured item. Place it again to retain its advert. Mining any part or removing its wall support also removes the complete board and returns one item. A normal wrench click rotates only where the new rectangle has suitable wall support and clear, permitted space.

Craft a Small board from iron nuggets, paper and a glass pane. Upgrade it to Large using iron ingots, paper and glass panes; upgrade Large to Landscape with further framing materials. See [the crafting guide](Crafting.md) for the exact grids. Selecting another advert costs no additional materials.

The twenty campaigns mix travel, village services and humour: Overworld Holidays, Creeper Cover Insurance, Emerald Mutual, Elytra Airways, Redstone Engineering, Bucket & Spade Coastal Breaks, Golden Carrot Café, Ender Express Parcels, Ironclad Workwear, The Nether Tourist Board, Bee & Blossom Gardens, SlimeCo Adhesives, Deep Dark Quiet Retreats, Oak & Acorn Homes, Diamond Standard Tools, Chicken Tonight Deliveries, Potion & Pestle, Shulker Storage Solutions, Village Careers Service and Overworld Rail.

Each advert has an original retained illustration and three proportionate layouts. Headers and taglines are typeset from the approved catalogue. The frames share the approved silver steel and use ordinary world lighting. The existing decorative Station Poster Case remains compatible.
