# Ground shunt signals

Four ground position-light repeaters are available: red/white and yellow/white, each with standard glass or LED lenses.

Two horizontal red or yellow lamps are restrictive; two diagonal white lamps show proceed. The yellow version does not grant a permissive yard movement. Create retains all movement authority.

Use the Railway Linking Tool to select the Create signal, then link the shunt within 128 blocks in the same dimension. Only a resolved green source shows white proceed. Mixed, red, invalid, pending, unloaded or missing sources remain restrictive. These signals do not look ahead beyond their linked source.

Ground-mounted housings stand on two short metal legs, raised a quarter block for visibility. Place on the ground, walls/brackets, ceilings or an existing wide pole. Normal Create wrench use rotates in 45-degree steps; sneak-wrench picks up the block with its settings. An integrated pole is refunded separately.

Craft the red standard shunt with an iron ingot, red stained glass, white stained glass and redstone. Add yellow dye to a red standard shunt for the yellow version. Upgrade either standard shunt with glowstone dust and redstone for its LED version. All recipes are shapeless.

The network map lists these under Repeaters, including their linked source and aspect. Native gameplay, shaders and multiplayer should be checked with the updated JAR on both client and server.

Wall-mounted shunts are centred on their mounting bracket; pole-mounted shunt hardware follows the raised casing. All standard/LED red/yellow shunt variants use compact 1.25-model-unit curved hoods. Ground legs and mainline hoods are preserved.

Shunt housings now have the approved asymmetric shoulder profile and a left-offset upper lamp. All red/yellow standard/LED variants retain their aspects, IDs, links and mounts. The clear indication uses the upper lamp and opposite lower white lamp. Selection/collision follows the shoulder profile.
