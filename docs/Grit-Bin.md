# Grit Bin

`ukrailways:grit_bin` is a decorative yellow grit bin with a closed lid, rear hinges, front latch, moulded inset panels and three feet. Its approximately waist-high model fits inside one block. It appears in the station creative catalogue, faces the player on placement, supports waterlogging and Create wrench rotation/pickup, and drops itself when broken. It has no inventory or opening interaction.

Craft one from eight yellow terracotta in a hollow square (`YYY / Y.Y / YYY`); the recipe book unlocks when yellow terracotta is obtained.

The lid uses a broad flat moulding to keep its surface uninterrupted at Minecraft scale. Offline mesh/texture inspection and automated resource checks are complete; native gameplay/shader inspection remains pending.
