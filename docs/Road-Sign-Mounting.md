# Road sign mounting

Narrow Poles are round, stackable silver supports. Their 32-facet surface, thin sign backs, clamps and bracket arms all sample the Wide Pole's existing `railway_atlas` steel region. They use ordinary supplied world light and emit no light. Larger boards have horizontal backing rails.

Craft six pole sections from three vertical iron nuggets. Three horizontal iron ingots make four Blank Road Signs. Configure a blank or an existing sign in the Roadworks Workbench, then use it on a Narrow Pole. The attachment faces the builder in eight directions. Click higher or lower on the section to set its mounting height. More signs can occupy the same section where their physical faces and brackets fit; supplementary plates can attach to sections below the main sign. Use a sign on a solid wall face for a wall mount.

A normal Create wrench click on a sign rotates that sign by 45 degrees. Sneak-wrench the face to retrieve its configured item, preserving its design and text. Sneak-wrench the bare pole to retrieve that section and its own attachments. If another section's wide board crosses the pole cell, remove that sign first. The other pole sections remain in place.

Every attachment has a unique owner identity and preserves its validated configuration. Empty occupied cells are explicit auxiliary blocks referencing the owner, without owning a second drop. Mining an auxiliary cell removes only the sign using that cell and keeps its pole. Breaking a host returns its pole and each owned sign exactly once; unrelated attachments remain. Empty auxiliary cells disappear. Periodic reconciliation repairs references after adjacent chunks reload, without forcing those chunks to load. Placement, rotation and wrench pickup check the complete loaded footprint, world border, build height, player permissions and protection events before mutation. Placement reserves air; it does not replace adjacent buildings or other materials.

The item and world renderer use the catalogue's individual face texture, retained physical outline and normalized UVs. Ear-clipped triangles retain rounded warning shoulders, pointed boards and concave crossbuck cut-outs; collision strips preserve every separate arm interval. Circles, GIVE WAY triangles, STOP octagons and tram diamonds have matching thin backing silhouettes. Changed editable fields cover only their defined regions. Unchanged fields retain the original artwork's lettering. Minecraft's Unicode font renders configured text, scaled to fit each bounded region.

The dependency-free `RoadSignGeometryCheck` checks smooth physical circle outlines, triangle orientation, STOP corners, opposing face separation and diagonal board extents. `RoadSignGameTests` exercises native ownership, configured text persistence, opposing signs, column stacks, supplementary plates, wall support, footprint mining/drop counts, protection callbacks, chunk boundaries, occupied-space refusal and wrench transactions. Register the module with `RoadSignContent.register(bus)` and client rendering with `RoadSignClient.register(bus)`; the module registers its native tests when the existing `ukrailways.trainKitTests` flag is enabled.
